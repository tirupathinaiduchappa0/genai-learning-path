## WHY THIS STRUCTURE WORKS (the senior signal)

| Part | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| Name + 4 yrs + current role | Crisp anchor — no rambling |
| Java → GenAI arc | **Growth story** — full-stack foundation + AI depth |
| "three main areas" | **Signposting** — he can follow you; sounds organized |
| MCP / attributes / DocSage | **Real production work** first, personal project last |
| "I enjoy agentic + RAG" | **Connects to the role** — ends with intent, not a trail-off |

> The magic word is **"three main areas"** — it tells the listener a list is coming, so your intro feels structured instead of a run-on.

---

## YOUR QUESTION: is DocSage the right thing to include?

**Yes — keep it, but order it LAST (as you do above).** Here's the logic:

- **MCP server + product attributes = real, client/production work.** Lead with these — they prove you ship AI in a live ERP system. That's what a Senior Associate is hired for.
- **DocSage = self-driven project.** Placing it last shows **initiative and depth** ("out of my own interest") without overshadowing your production work.
- Don't replace DocSage with "a RAG directly in our app" — DocSage is your *strongest architecture story* (LangGraph, agentic, self-correction). It's exactly what an architect round wants. It's also the project he'll most likely ask you to "walk through" (Lesson 22).

> Lead with production (MCP + attributes), close with the impressive personal build (DocSage). Best of both.

---

## DELIVERY RULES (this is where your draft loses points)

1. **Slow down.** Your draft is one breathless sentence. Pause at every `||`. Silence is fine.
2. **Three areas = three clear blocks.** Say "First… Second… Third…" out loud. It forces structure.
3. **Don't over-explain in the intro.** Give the headline; let him ask for depth. Each of the 3 areas is a *door* he can open.
4. **End with intent**, not a trail-off ("…that is what from my introduction side" → cut this). Land on "what drew me to this role."
5. **Numbers, not vague.** "4 years," "two stages," "each endpoint as a tool" — specifics = credibility.

---

## WHAT EACH AREA WILL TRIGGER (be ready to go deeper)

- **MCP server** → "What is MCP? Why expose endpoints as tools? What's field-level tokenization?"
- **Product attributes** → "Why two stages? How do you prevent duplicates? How do you map?"
- **DocSage** → "Walk me through the architecture." → **Lesson 22 spine** carries you.

> Each headline in your intro is a promise. Have one deeper paragraph ready for each — but only say it when asked.
