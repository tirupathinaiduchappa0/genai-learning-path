# "Tell me about Infor / your product" — Interview Answer Card

> Interviewers ask this to check you understand the BUSINESS context, not just code. A crisp answer = you're engaged with what you build, not just coding in isolation. Facts below are accurate as of 2026.

---

## FAST FACTS (accurate — use confidently)

- **What:** Infor is a multinational **enterprise software company** — industry-specific **cloud ERP**.
- **HQ:** New York City, USA.
- **Owner:** A standalone subsidiary of **Koch Industries** (acquired 2020).
- **Scale:** **68,000+ customers** globally; ~$4B invested in product R&D.
- **Flagship:** **Infor CloudSuite** — cloud ERP **purpose-built per industry** (not one generic ERP configured later).
- **Runs on:** AWS, via the **Infor OS** platform (includes **Coleman AI** and **Birst** analytics).
- **Industries served:** manufacturing, **distribution**, healthcare, food & beverage, fashion/retail, public sector, automotive, aerospace, hospitality, chemicals.
- **Differentiator vs SAP/Oracle:** Oracle/SAP give one platform configured per industry; **Infor ships pre-built industry editions** (deep "last-mile" fit, less customization needed).
- **Direction now:** pushing the **"agentic enterprise"** — embedding **GenAI, AI agents, and RPA** directly into the ERP. (This is exactly why your GenAI work matters to them.)

---

## YOUR SPECIFIC PRODUCT (the strong, concrete detail)

You work on **Infor Distribution SX.e** — the ERP for **wholesale distributors**.

> "My team works on **Infor Distribution SX.e**, which is Infor's ERP for wholesale distribution businesses. It handles the core operations of a distributor — **inventory and stock management, sales order creation, purchasing, pricing, and customer/vendor management**. My focus is on **embedding GenAI capabilities** into that product."

This is gold: it's specific, it's real, and it ties your AI work to a clear business domain.

## LIKELY FOLLOW-UPS → ONE-LINE ANCHORS

- **"What does your product do exactly?"** → "SX.e runs a distributor's operations — inventory/stock, sales orders, purchasing, pricing, customer & vendor management."
- **"Who are the customers?"** → "Wholesale distribution businesses, largely North America; mid-market to upper mid-market."
- **"How does your AI work fit in?"** → "Infor's strategy is the 'agentic enterprise' — I build the MCP server and agents that embed GenAI into the ERP, e.g. natural-language access to orders/inventory and product-data enrichment."
- **"Infor vs SAP/Oracle?"** → "Oracle/SAP configure one platform per industry; Infor ships pre-built industry editions with deeper out-of-the-box fit and less customization."
- **"What's Coleman AI?"** → "Infor's AI/ML layer in the Infor OS platform — used for predictions and recommendations across CloudSuites." (Mention only if asked; don't overclaim.)

---

## HONESTY GUARDRAILS (don't overclaim)

- Stick to **what your team builds** (MCP server, agents, AI features on SX.e). Don't claim to know all of Infor's internal roadmap.
- If asked something company-wide you don't know: *"I'm focused on the GenAI side of SX.e, so I can't speak to that area in detail."* — clean and confident.
- Don't quote revenue/financials you're unsure of. The facts above (HQ, owner, 68k customers, CloudSuite, industries) are safe.
